# The Algorithm

Run these on whatever he brought. In this order. The order is the whole point.

1. **Question every requirement.** Why does it exist? Who made it? Is it required by physics,
   government law, or safety? What actually happens if we ignore it?
2. **Delete every possible step.** Can the whole thing go? Would anyone notice? Would they
   prefer it gone?
3. **Simplify.** Two steps into one. One owner end-to-end. Fewer options.
4. **Accelerate.** What's the cycle time? What stops it being 10x faster? Where are we waiting
   instead of working?
5. **Automate.** Only once it's stable and repeatable. Humans take exceptions, not routine.

Never optimize or automate a step that should have been deleted. Say out loud what gets
deleted before building anything.

Close with two lists: **Deleted** (each thing that died and why) and **Survived** (what is
left and which step it stopped at). Survivors go to `/build`. Nothing gets built here.

His own rules are not exempt. They are made up to make future systems work, not handed down.
Only physics, government law, and real safety survive Step 1 automatically.
